\section{Verification purpose}

\textbf{Case ID:} \texttt{\CaseID}. This case verifies the coupled surface-fire and level-set rate response to a central point ignition in uniform fuel with zero wind and zero slope. Rotational symmetry supplies a diagnostic for directional bias or raster-orientation errors, while the formal decision tests the coupling between fire behavior and level-set normal velocity. The verification question is whether the required maximum spread rate is within 10 percent of \(1.51~\mathrm{m\,min^{-1}}\); isotropic arrival geometry is reported as supporting evidence but is not yet a separate acceptance component.

\section{Mathematical formulation and numerical implementation}

ELMFIRE advances the level-set field using \(\partial\phi/\partial t+V_n|\nabla\phi|=0\).  With spatially uniform fuel, moisture, wind, and slope, \(V_n=R_0\) is independent of azimuth.  The zero contour must therefore evolve from the ignition neighborhood toward a circle.  The final \texttt{vs} raster records the local normal spread rate in metres per minute because \texttt{SPREAD\_RATE\_IN\_M} is enabled.

\section{Derivation of expected behavior}

For constant \(R_0\), the front radius is \(r(t)=r(0)+R_0t\).  The independently tabulated Fuel Model 3 response for the configured moisture and no-wind/no-slope condition is \(R_0=1.51~\mathrm{m\,min^{-1}}\).  Rotational symmetry is an invariant; the quantitative decision uses the rate because it directly tests the fire-behavior value passed to propagation.

\section{Verification metrics and acceptance criteria}

The head-ROS metric is the maximum finite value in the required final \texttt{vs} raster.  It is selected because uniform forcing makes every mature front direction equivalent and the maximum avoids dilution by unburned nodata cells.  A 10 percent relative tolerance follows the GUIDE comparison and allows raster and front-discretization effects while remaining sensitive to unit or coupling errors.  The case passes only when the output exists and \(|R_{\rm sim}-1.51|/1.51\le0.10\).

\section{Simulation configuration and predicted outcome}

The case uses a \(126\times126\), 30 m grid in EPSG:32635, uniform Fuel Model 3, 6 percent one-hour moisture, flat terrain, zero wind, and a central ignition.  The predicted output is an approximately circular arrival-time field with a uniform mature spread rate near \(1.51~\mathrm{m\,min^{-1}}\).

\section{Technical interpretation}

A rate failure indicates a fire-behavior, units, or output-selection problem. Strong directional distortion with an acceptable maximum is a diagnostic warning and must not be claimed as verified until a shape metric and tolerance are added. No scientific conclusion should be drawn when the status is NOT RUN or NOT EVALUABLE.

\section{Scope and limitations}

This is deterministic code verification for one fuel and moisture state. It does not validate Fuel Model 3 against experimental fires or quantify symmetry with a dedicated shape metric. This case provides implementation verification evidence only.
