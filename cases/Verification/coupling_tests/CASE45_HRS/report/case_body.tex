\section{Verification purpose}

\textbf{Case ID:} \texttt{\CaseID}.  This manufactured coupling test verifies the
WU--E model's conversion of heat received by an urban cell into its local
level-set rate of spread.  It exercises \texttt{UMD\_UCB\_BLDG\_SPREAD} and the
subsequent directional velocity construction in
\texttt{COMPUTE\_SPREAD\_VELOCITIES}. The intended oracle uses a fixed
material \texttt{FTP\_CRIT}; it is sensitive to energy or unit normalization,
ellipse partitioning, normal-vector orientation, an artificial 30,000-kJ or
35-mph discontinuity, and failure to honor the material table. The source
revision reviewed for the contract is
\SourceCommit.

The design review did not use a globally clean checkout.  The five reviewed
files enumerated in \texttt{case.yaml} were byte-identical to that commit;
their SHA-256 digests are recorded there.  At review time
\texttt{elmfire.f90}, \texttt{elmfire\_io.f90}, and
\texttt{elmfire\_subs.f90} were modified and untracked entries were also
present.  Those paths were outside the reviewed file set.  No case runtime
script inspects the checkout, and the recorded commit does not by itself
authenticate a subsequently supplied executable.

One burning urban source and one adjacent, initially positive urban receiver
form the measured pair in a nonburnable domain.  Four disconnected urban guard
cells keep the tagged-list termination guard inactive but remain far from the
front and are not observables.  Per-step energy, wind, receiver normal,
design-fire amplitude, and table \texttt{FTP\_CRIT} are varied; spotting,
crown fire, terrain, moisture gradients, and stochastic forcing are disabled.
This is implementation verification, not evidence that the empirical response
is physically valid.  The verification question is: for all 12 variants, does
the executable's receiver-cell spread-rate raster agree within
\ROSTolerance{} relative error with the independently recomputed mapping driven
by its own recorded DFC-plus-radiation stimulus, while also satisfying the
continuity, directional, amplitude, and FTP-response comparisons?


\paragraph{Reading the notation.}
WU--E denotes the Wildland--Urban ELMFIRE model. HRR is heat-release rate;
HRRPUA is heat-release rate per unit area. DFC means direct flame contact,
TOA means time of arrival, and ROS means rate of spread. FTP is the material
fire-thermal property used in the intended heat-to-spread law.

\section{Mathematical formulation and numerical implementation}

At one receiver the executable records transient heat fluxes
\(q''_{\DFC}\) and \(q''_{\rm rad}\) in \si{\kilo\watt\per\square\metre}.
For timestep \(\Delta t\) and square cell width \(\Delta x\), the downstream
routine forms
\[
 E=(q''_{\DFC}+q''_{\rm rad})\Delta t\Delta x^2,
 \qquad q_A=E/\Delta x^2 .
\]
Because kilowatts are kilojoules per second, \(E\) is in kilojoules. The
intended downscaled model uses the material value $F_{TP,crit}$ without an
energy-dependent replacement or wind-speed divisor. The absolute velocity in
feet per minute is
\[
 U=\min\!\left(10^5,
 \frac{60E}{0.3048\,\Delta t\,\Delta x\,F_{TP,crit}}\right)
 =\min\!\left(10^5,\frac{196.85\,q''\Delta x}{F_{TP,crit}}\right).
\]
The nominal default is $F_{TP,crit}=5400~\si{\kilo\joule\per\square\metre}$;
the case also uses 3000 and 6000 to verify the inverse response. The
peer-reviewed papers describe the accumulated-heat/FTP ignition and
heat-derived urban spread concept \cite{PurnomoPCI2024,PurnomoIJWF2024}.
The pinned source instead replaces FTP below/above 30,000 kJ and applies a
1.8 divisor at and below 35 mph. Those operations are documented deviations,
not acceptance equations.

The UCB ellipse supplies major radius \(a\), minor radius \(b\), and focus
offset \(e\).  The three response weights are
\[
 f_h=a+e,\qquad f_s=2b,\qquad f_b=a-e,
\]
so the nominal head, side, and back velocities are
\(V_i=Uf_i/(f_h+f_s+f_b)\).  The case-local evaluator then applies the same
closed ellipse-normal construction, rather than treating these three labels as
three scalar shortcuts.  In full, for front normal
\(\boldsymbol n=(n_x,n_y)\) and direction-of-maximum-spread unit vector
\(\boldsymbol s=(s_x,s_y)\), the implementation evaluates
\[
 c=n_xs_x+n_ys_y,\qquad s=n_xs_y-n_ys_x,
\]
\[
 L=\begin{cases}
 \min\!\left((V_h+V_b)/(2V_s),10\right),&V_s>10^{-4},\\
 1,&V_s\leq10^{-4},
 \end{cases}
\]
\[
 A=\max\!\left(\frac{V_h+V_b}{2},10^{-10}\right),\qquad
 B=\frac12\max\!\left(\frac{V_h+V_b}{L},10^{-10}\right),
\]
\[
 D=\max\!\left(\sqrt{A^2c^2+B^2s^2},10^{-10}\right),\quad
 v_{\parallel}=\frac{A^2c}{D}+\frac{V_h-V_b}{2},\quad
 v_{\perp}=\frac{B^2s}{D},
\]
\[
 v_x=v_{\parallel}s_x+v_{\perp}s_y,\qquad
 v_y=v_{\parallel}s_y-v_{\perp}s_x,\qquad
 V=\sqrt{v_x^2+v_y^2}.
\]
The flat grid makes the source projection factors unity.  Wind is from
\(270^\circ\), hence \(\boldsymbol s=(1,0)\).  The manufactured target normals
are east (head), west (back), and north (side); raster rows run north to south
while the stored mathematical normal is expressed in east/north coordinates.

The upstream heat values are recorded stimuli for this case; CASE43--44 verify
their governing equations. Here \texttt{BLDG\_AREA}=20 denotes the source's
linear characteristic building dimension in metres, not an area in square
metres. The input generator chooses design-fire amplitudes deterministically,
but the downstream oracle always uses the heat actually written by ELMFIRE.
For traceability, the pinned source currently uses the following non-normative
normalization while constructing that stimulus:
\[
 C_H=\frac{\Delta x^2}{\pi(ca)(cb)},\qquad c=0.5.
\]
For target distance \(R\) and directional ellipse distance \(R_e\), the input
generator transcribes the overlap and radiation weights
\[
 C_D=\operatorname{clip}_{[0,1]}\left(\frac{R_e+\Delta x/2-R}{\Delta x}\right),
 \qquad
 C_R=(1-C_D)\operatorname{clip}_{[0,1]}\left(\frac{R_e+100+\Delta x/2-R}{\Delta x}\right).
\]
With the implementation's piecewise effective radiation distance
\(R_{\rm eff}\), emissivity \(\varepsilon=0.89\), and radiative fraction 0.3,
the planned plateau heat is
\[
 q''=C_H H_p\left[C_D+\frac{0.3\,\varepsilon C_R\Delta x^2}
 {4\pi R_{\rm eff}^2}\right].
\]
The generator solves this relation separately for each normal to set the input
amplitude.  The downstream decision never substitutes planned heat for the
recorded heat.

\section{Derivation of expected behavior}

The energy triplet is constructed at \(29900\), \(30000\), and \(30100\) kJ.
The generator uses an explicit IEEE-754 binary32 transcription of the reviewed
default-\texttt{REAL} operation sequence to select decimal table amplitudes;
the planned realization is exact at all three values, including equality, and
is recorded in each variant manifest. Executable heat output remains required
to establish the realized stimulus. With fixed FTP, local speed divided by
recorded energy must be continuous across 30,000 kJ. Likewise, the 34.9,
35.0, and 35.1-mph cases include the small physical ellipse change but no
artificial divisor jump. A discontinuity at either boundary identifies the
known source-only branches.

The terminal spread-rate raster is populated only from \texttt{LIST\_BURNED},
so an arbitrary positive receiver would not be observable.  The prepared
receiver value is instead $\phi_r=0.001>0$. Both source raster readers
unconditionally add a perturbation $10^{-3}(0.5-R)$, with $R\in[0,1)$;
wind-fluctuation settings do not disable it. Thus the receiver remains
strictly positive, between 0.0005 and 0.0015, before propagation. The former
$2^{-20}$ value was smaller than this perturbation and could not guarantee
initial state. For the side receiver, its five-cell tangential PHI stencil is
flat at $\phi_r$, removing an artificial tangential local minimum. These
four extra stencil cells remain nonburnable and initially positive.

The crossing estimate uses $\boldsymbol{v}\cdot\nabla\phi$, not speed
magnitude times a one-dimensional gradient. Its source-facing Superbee
gradient is $G=2(\phi_r-\phi_s)/\Delta x=0.0451$ per metre.
With $\epsilon=0.0005$, the generator checks the conservative first-stage
bound
\[
 \tfrac12\Delta t\left[v_n(G-4\epsilon/\Delta x)
       - U(4\epsilon/\Delta x)\right]>\phi_r+\epsilon,
\]
where $v_n$ is normal velocity and $U$ bounds the velocity magnitude, both
in metres per second. The bound covers input-noise gradients and discards
second-stage inward transport. It is an intended-law setup check; actual
arrival remains independently required. All twelve receivers must enter
\texttt{LIST\_BURNED} at exactly 1~s, with a finite terminal speed.
Four positive guard cells
keep more than two nodes tagged, and a scheduled ignition at 5~s---strictly
after the 4~s stop---keeps the first-step stall predicate false without ever
altering the simulated state.

At fixed geometry, doubling versus halving \(H_p\) gives a heat-flux ratio of
four. The two \texttt{FTP\_CRIT} variants use 3000 and 6000
$\si{\kilo\joule\per\square\metre}$ and are otherwise equivalent. Their heat
fields must match, while their spread speeds must have the inverse FTP ratio
$V_{3000}/V_{6000}=2$. The
\texttt{wind\_equal\_35}, \texttt{normal\_back}, and
\texttt{normal\_side} variants share 35-mph wind and the same designed received
energy; they are the matched head, back, and side comparison, leaving only the
prescribed receiver normal to select the directional response.

This is a recorded-input oracle: the emitted/received heat field at the exact
receiver-arrival dump is a measured stimulus, while only the downstream
heat-to-ROS calculation is the object of the numerical comparison.  It
therefore avoids claiming that one executable
output independently verifies another upstream heat calculation.  Ellipse
axes, \(E\) as held in working storage, \(F\), absolute \(U\), component
velocities, and \texttt{LOW} are internal-only quantities.  They are calculated
by the evaluator but cannot be asserted as separately observed executable
outputs.

\section{Verification metrics and acceptance criteria}

The postprocessor first recomputes the SHA-256 fingerprint of every generated
runtime namelist, GeoTIFF, and local table.  Each variant must also have a
matching completion marker written only after a successful ELMFIRE exit.  That
marker binds the runtime fingerprint, hashed \texttt{expected.json}, evaluator
hash, exact variant metadata, resolved executable path and hash, stdout/stderr
snapshot, and complete output-file snapshot.  All 12 markers must identify one
binary path/hash/size tuple.  Any later change invalidates the run evidence.

With \texttt{DUMP\_EVERY\_STEP}, the exact ledger is dump indices 1--5 at
times 0, 1, 2, 3, and 4~s, with only index 5 final.  Transient heat filenames
use those dump indices.  In contrast, terminal TOA and spread-rate filenames
use the numeric time stamp \texttt{0000004}; the evaluator never mistakes final
dump index 5 for that time stamp.  It selects DFC plus radiation at the unique
dump whose time equals the receiver's required 1-s TOA, and compares the
resulting independent local-speed oracle with the terminal receiver speed
captured when that cell entered \texttt{LIST\_BURNED}.  The exact-stop transient
heat values must be reset to zero.  A missing or stale marker, fingerprint or
snapshot mismatch, nodata, nonfinite, missing, duplicate, stale, or off-time
artifact makes the case NOT EVALUABLE.

\section{Technical interpretation}

The repaired receiver stencil provides complete, observable arrival and speed
evidence. Its positive initial PHI remains positive under the source's input
noise, and its side-normal transport no longer competes with an artificial
tangential minimum. The observability result therefore separates a usable
experiment from agreement with the intended spread law.

The remaining failures identify the heat-to-speed mapping. The source replaces
the table FTP with an energy-dependent value and applies a separate wind-speed
divisor. The material-pair speeds consequently do not show the required inverse
FTP response. The energy and wind comparisons expose the associated branch
changes, while the correct HRR amplitude ratio confirms that the prescribed
source-amplitude control was realized. See the generated metric rows for the
measured errors and decisions; a complete run is not a scientific PASS.

\section{Scope and limitations}

The case verifies the executable-visible local speed produced by one WU--E
urban receiver under a compact deterministic sweep.  It does not independently
verify the upstream heat-flux model, ignition delay over multiple cells, accumulated exposure across
timesteps, building-to-building community spread, or convergence.  Internal
working variables listed above remain inferred from the reviewed formulation,
not observed.  The case binds one resolved executable path and SHA-256 to all
variant inputs, logs, oracle metadata, and output snapshots, but a binary hash
does not prove that the binary was built from the pinned review commit.  The
result supplies code-verification evidence only.
